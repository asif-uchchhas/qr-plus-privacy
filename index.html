Welcome to QR Plus - Scan & Create ("QR Plus").

QR Plus is a QR code utility application that allows users to scan QR codes, create QR codes, save QR-related information locally, view history, and share QR-related content.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains what information QR Plus accesses, how it is used, where it is stored, and when it may be shared.

1. Information We Access

Camera

QR Plus requests access to your device camera when you use the QR scanning functionality.

The camera is used to:

  • Scan and recognize QR codes and supported barcode formats.
  • Process visual information necessary to identify QR code content.

QR Plus does not use the camera to continuously monitor you or record video for unrelated purposes.

Photos and Media

If you choose to select an image from your device for QR scanning or another supported feature, QR Plus may request access to the relevant media on your device.

This access is initiated by your action and is used only to perform the selected feature.

QR Code Content

QR Plus may process information contained in QR codes that you scan or create. Depending on what you scan or enter, this information may include:

  • Website URLs
  • Text
  • Contact information
  • Email addresses
  • Telephone numbers
  • Wi-Fi information
  • Other information encoded in a QR code
Important: QR Plus does not determine or control what information is contained in a QR code. Users should avoid scanning or storing sensitive information if they do not want that information retained on their device.

2. Local Storage

QR Plus may store QR-related information, including scan or creation history and application settings, locally on your device.

This local information is used to provide features such as:

  • Scan history
  • QR creation history
  • Application preferences and settings
  • Previously generated QR information

QR Plus does not intentionally transmit this locally stored information to our servers for the purpose of providing these features.

3. Sharing QR Content

QR Plus may provide sharing functionality that allows you to share QR codes or QR-related content using applications available on your device.

When you choose to share information, the information is transferred through the application or service you select.

You are responsible for choosing the recipient and the third-party application or service used for sharing.

QR Plus does not control the privacy practices of third-party applications that you choose to use for sharing.

4. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

QR Plus does not require you to create an account to use its core functionality.

In the current version of QR Plus, we do not intentionally collect personal information such as:

  • Your name
  • Home address
  • Government identification
  • Passwords
  • Contacts
  • Precise location
  • Financial or payment information

5. Internet and Third-Party Services

QR Plus may interact with external services when you voluntarily use functionality that requires an external application or website, such as opening a URL contained in a QR code.

When you open an external website or use a third-party application, that service may collect information according to its own privacy policy.

QR Plus is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites, applications, or services.

6. Advertising

The current version of QR Plus does not intentionally display third-party advertising.

If advertising or advertising-related services are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the relevant Google Play Data Safety information will be updated as necessary.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by QR Plus.

QR-related history and application settings are primarily stored locally on your device. However, no electronic storage system or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

QR Plus does not require an online user account.

Information stored locally by the application remains on your device until you delete it through available application functionality, clear the application's data, or uninstall the application.

Uninstalling QR Plus generally removes application data stored in the application's private storage according to Android's standard application data-management behavior.

Information that you voluntarily share with another application or external service is subject to that service's own retention and deletion policies.

9. Children's Privacy

QR Plus is not specifically directed toward children.

QR Plus does not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account-registration system because QR Plus does not require user accounts.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us using the contact information below.

10. Third-Party Services

QR Plus may use third-party software libraries and Android services to provide application functionality.

Such third parties may process technical information necessary to provide their services. Their processing is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

We aim to use third-party components in accordance with applicable Google Play requirements.

11. Your Choices

You control whether to grant QR Plus access to permissions.

You can:

  • Deny camera access.
  • Change application permissions through Android Settings.
  • Delete locally stored QR history where available.
  • Clear the application's data.
  • Uninstall QR Plus.

Some functionality may not work if the required permission is denied.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when QR Plus functionality, data practices, third-party services, or applicable requirements change.

When changes are made, we will update the Last Updated date at the top of this page.
